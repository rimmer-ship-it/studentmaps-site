Student Maps Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 20, 2026
Last updated: February 20, 2026

Student Maps ("Student Maps", "we", "us", "our") operates the Student Maps iOS application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Email address (required to create and access your account).
  • Display name (optional).
  • Profile information you choose to add (for example, an avatar if you upload one).

Content you create in the App

  • Spots you suggest or edit (such as name, category/type, tags, and coordinates).
  • Votes (for example, tag voting and spot/venue voting).
  • Reviews and ratings, if you submit them.

Location data

  • If you grant permission, the App can access your device location to center the map and show nearby spots.
  • We do not need location permission to browse the App, but some features may work better with it enabled.

Technical and security data

  • Basic diagnostic and security information required to keep the service reliable (for example, timestamps of actions and system logs).

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To show spots on the map and enable filters based on tags, type, and opening hours.
  • To power community features such as tag voting, spot/venue voting, reviews, and favorites.
  • To maintain the integrity of the platform (prevent abuse, spam, and fraud).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

3. Service providers and third parties

We use service providers to operate the App. These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the service:

  • Supabase (backend hosting, database, and authentication). Supabase may process personal data as described in its privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy.
  • Apple and Google (only if you choose Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In). These providers process sign-in information according to their own policies.
  • Apple MapKit (map rendering). We use maps for visualization; we do not rely on Apple Points of Interest data.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Contract: to provide the App features you request (account, community actions).
  • Consent: for optional permissions such as location access (you can change this in iOS Settings).
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and maintain the App, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

5. Data retention

  • We keep account and community content for as long as your account is active.
  • You can request deletion of your account; when deleted, we remove or anonymize personal data where applicable.
  • Some security and operational logs may be retained for a limited period to prevent abuse and maintain service integrity.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can update certain profile details in the App.
  • You can control location permissions via iOS Settings.
  • If you are in the EEA/UK, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to request a copy of your data.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect data against unauthorized access.

8. Children

Student Maps is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are significant, we will update the date at the top.

10. Contact

If you have questions or requests about privacy, contact us at: privacy@studentmaps.nl
